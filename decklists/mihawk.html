Mihawk

Green Mihawk. Gain power against Slash Leaders, then rest a card to refresh DON!! if a 5-cost Character is in play.

Green Leader OP14-020 5 Life 5000 Power Slash The Seven Warlords of the Sea
If your opponent's Leader has the <Slash> attribute, this Leader gains +1000 power. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may rest 1 of your cards: If there is a Character with a cost of 5 or more, set up to 3 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, you cannot play Character cards during this turn.

Green Mihawk decklist by Mike - 23rd Place Treasure Cup Wolverhampton - 19th July 2026
