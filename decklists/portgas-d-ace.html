Portgas D. Ace

Red OP16 Portgas D. Ace. Once per turn, give Rush to a Monkey D. Luffy character or a Whitebeard Pirates character with 8000 power or more.

Red Leader OP16-001 5 Life 5000 Power Special Whitebeard Pirates
[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Up to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] Characters or up to 1 of your Characters with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates", with 8000 power or more, gains [Rush] during this turn.

Red OP16 Ace. The red/blue OP13 Ace page is separate.

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Red OP16 Portgas D. Ace is Whitebeard rush — not the red/blue OP13 Ace. Lists lock 4 Monkey D. Luffy, 4 Edward Newgate, 4 Vista, and Moby Dick; Garp, Little Oars Jr., Curiel, Marco, and Thatch are close to required. The 10-cost Ace is usually a 3-of; Namule, Time for the Counterattack, Uta, and Izo are flex.
