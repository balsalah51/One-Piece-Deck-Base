RG Luffy

Red/Green Leader OP13-001 4 Life 5000 Power Strike Supernovas/Straw Hat Crew
[DON!! x1] [On Your Opponent's Attack] If you have 5 or less active DON!! cards, you may rest any number of your DON!! cards. For every DON!! card rested this way, this Leader or up to 1 of your {Straw Hat Crew} type Characters gains +2000 power during this battle.

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

RG Luffy is a Straw Hat value pile that still posts in current format. Lists average Sanji, Usopp, Nami, EB04 Zoro, Brook, Charlestone, starter Luffy, and Thousand Sunny. Laboon, Electrical Luna, and Bonney are common; the 10-cost Luffy and extra Zoro events are the usual cuts.
