Charlotte Katakuri

Purple OP11 Charlotte Katakuri. Once per turn on attack or on your opponent's attack, DON!! −1 to look at the top of their deck and gain power.

Purple Leader OP11-062 5 Life 5000 Power Strike Big Mom Pirates
[When Attacking]/[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1: Look at 1 card from the top of your opponent's deck. Then, this Leader gains +1000 power during this battle.

Purple OP11 Katakuri. Not yellow OP17 Linlin.

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Purple OP11 Charlotte Katakuri is the other Big Mom leader, separate from yellow OP17 Linlin. DON!! −1 on attack or on the opponent's attack to peek their deck and gain power. Core is Katakuri and Pudding in both printings plus ST34 Big Mom: Katakuri, Brulee, Linlin, and Cracker, with We're Going to Claim the One Piece, Mamaragan, and Divine Departure.
