Charlotte Katakuri

Purple OP11 Charlotte Katakuri. Once per turn on attack or on your opponent's attack, DON!! −1 to look at the top of their deck and gain power.

Purple Leader OP11-062 5 Life 5000 Power Strike Big Mom Pirates
[When Attacking]/[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1: Look at 1 card from the top of your opponent's deck. Then, this Leader gains +1000 power during this battle.

Current-format leader. Tournament 50-card lists from Limitless Play are below.

Community take

YouTube and X

Purple OP11 Charlotte Katakuri is the other Big Mom leader people still register, separate from yellow OP17 Linlin. The effect is small and repeatable: DON!! −1 on attack or on the opponent's attack to peek their deck and gain power. The Limitless core is Katakuri and Pudding in both printings, plus the ST34 Big Mom package: Katakuri, Brulee, Linlin, and Cracker. We're Going to Claim the One Piece, Mamaragan, and Divine Departure show up in most lists. The consensus below is averaged from those public tournament lists.
