How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Purple/yellow OP09 Nico Robin is a banish leader that trashes a Trigger to rest a DON!!. Recent lists have picked up OP17 cards. This is the Straw Hat archaeologist leader, not a cameo in Black Luffy.
