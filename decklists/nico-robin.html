Nico Robin

Purple/Yellow OP09 Nico Robin. Banish damage, then trash a Trigger to rest a DON!!. Straw Hat archaeologist leader, not a cameo in Black Luffy.

[Banish] (When this card deals damage, the target card is trashed without activating its Trigger.) [When Attacking] You may trash 1 card with a [Trigger] from your hand: Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it.
