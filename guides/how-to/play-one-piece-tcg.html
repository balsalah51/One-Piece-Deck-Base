How to play One Piece TCG

Each player has a leader, a 50-card deck, a DON!! deck, and life. You attach DON!! to attack or activate effects. Official rules live on Bandai’s site. This fan site is for copying constructed 50-card lists.

FAQ

OPTCG

How many cards is an OPTCG deck?

50 cards plus a leader. That is the constructed format used in the lists here.

Where do I get a list?

Open a leader page, then a tournament or community row. Hover a name for the English picture.

Is this official?

No. Fan site, not affiliated with Bandai or Shueisha. Events stay on Bandai’s site.
