OPTCG rules

Constructed OPTCG: one leader, 50 cards, DON!! deck, life equal to the leader’s printed life. Keywords like Rush, Blocker, Banish, and Trigger are on the cards. For the full document, use Bandai. For lists, use a leader page here.

FAQ

OPTCG

What is DON!!?

The resource you attach each turn. Many leaders spend DON!! −1 or ask for DON!! x1.

What is a Trigger?

An effect that can happen when the card is taken as life. Some leaders, like Nico Robin, trash a Trigger from hand.
