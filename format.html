Standard format

Lists on this site are 50-card constructed decks plus a leader. English events right now are still the OP16 Standard pool. OP17 English is dated 28 August 2026.

Banned in constructed

Effective 1 April 2026

Bandai banned Blue Charlotte Pudding OP06-047, including parallels. It cannot be in any constructed deck. Other Pudding prints stay legal.

Official notice: Banned/Restricted Cards effective from April 1, 2026.

This site drops any scraped list that still plays OP06-047.

Block 1 rotation
